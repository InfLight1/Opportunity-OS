## Night Route · taste: what to improve
1. The route idea stops after the hero. Carry it through: the funnel and reuse should feel like stops and branches on one route.
2. The story sections have long empty stretches while scrolling (sticky panels with nothing in them). Tighten.
3. Too much tiny uppercase mono text (nav, labels, buttons). Hard to read; keep mono for numbers/dates only.
4. The city is very faint. It needs a real image later (hero slot) and a stronger glowing route over it.
5. Planner boxes all look the same (navy card, thin border). Focus vs Consider vs Not now need clearer visual weight.
6. Reuse "threads" read as a list; the threads themselves are barely visible.

## Unfair Advantage · impeccable: what to improve
1. (User) Hero chips are bunched in one stack at the bottom. Spread them out.
2. The small tick bars under hours on cards can read like a meter. Remove them (house rule: no meters).

5. Planner sections feel like one long dark page; add rhythm (section numbers, more space between parts).
